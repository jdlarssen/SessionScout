**Choose your available time. See up to three of your own tasks that fit.**

SessionScout helps turn a WoW to-do list into a plan for this session. Enter the
tasks you want to do, give them time estimates and priorities, then choose your
available minutes. It suggests up to three unfinished tasks whose combined
estimates fit, favoring higher priorities.

**For WoW Forever 1.60.1.** Version 0.1.0 separates **This session**, where you
choose your time and see suggestions, from **My tasks**, where you manage the
list. Estimates and completion are manual; the addon does not discover
activities, detect quest completion or calculate gear upgrades.

## Get started

1. Download [**SessionScout 0.1.0 — Forever**](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/sessionscout/files/9222143),
   also available from this project's **Files** tab.
   With WoW closed, extract the ZIP's `SessionScout` folder into your Forever
   `Interface/AddOns` folder. The resulting path must contain
   `SessionScout/SessionScout.toc` directly. Keep your SavedVariables when updating.
2. Start Forever, enable SessionScout in the AddOns menu and enter `/scout`.
3. Open **My tasks** → **+ New task**. Enter a task name and estimated minutes,
   choose its priority, repeat setting and completion scope, then press **Add task**.
4. Return to **This session**. Choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or enter 1–240 whole
   minutes in **Custom minutes** and press **Apply**. For two hours, enter `120`.
5. Mark a task **Done** when you finish. In **My tasks** → **Completed**, **Undo**
   makes it unfinished again. Leave a **Next time, remember...** note for your
   next session.

**Nothing fits?** A task estimated at 120 minutes will not appear in a 90-minute
plan. The planner shows the shortest unfinished estimate so you can adjust your
available time or edit an estimate that was inaccurate.

## What is saved

- One-time, daily and weekly tasks, plus completion and your selected budget.
- Shared task definitions: editing or deleting a task affects every character.
  Completion can be separate for each character or shared across the account.
- A next-session reminder for the current character.

Daily and weekly completion uses reset times supplied by the server. If the
required reset API is unavailable, SessionScout explains why completion could
not be saved; it does not guess a reset schedule.

There are no dependencies, external accounts or telemetry. General bag space
and lowest equipped-item durability are shown outside combat.

## Compatibility and feedback

The new controls were checked with mouse and keyboard in Forever 1.60.1.70450:
creating and editing tasks, cancelling edits, Done/Undo, confirmed deletion,
custom budgets and invalid input. Existing tasks, completion and character
settings were preserved after `/reload`. The release differs from the tested
alpha.4 package only in its version strings.

Actual reset crossings, multiple-character use, combat transitions, extreme UI
scales and long sessions still need testing. Retail and other Classic versions
are not supported by this file. Installation and updating through the
CurseForge app have not been verified; the manual installation steps above apply.
